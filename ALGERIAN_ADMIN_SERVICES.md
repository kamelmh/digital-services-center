# DSC Administrative Services — Research & Build Guide

## Overview

This document details every Algerian administrative service DSC can offer, with exact procedures, official forms, deadlines, penalties, and pricing strategy.

---

## 1. G12 — Déclaration Prévisionnelle IFU (Série G N°12)

### What It Is
Annual forecast declaration for the Impôt Forfaitaire Unique (IFU). Every business with CA < 8M DZD must file this to declare expected revenue and pay tax upfront.

### Who Must File
- Commerçants, industriels, artisans, professions libérales under IFU regime
- Auto-entrepreneurs (ANAE) with CA < 5M DZD
- Sociétés civiles professionnelles

### Deadlines
| Event | Deadline |
|-------|----------|
| G12 (forecast) | 30 June each year (2026: extended to 30 Sept) |
| G12 BIS (final) | 20 January N+1 |

### How to Fill (Cadre by Cadre)
**Recto:**
- Cadre I: Identity (NIF, NIN, name, address, activity)
- Cadre I: Check exemption box (ANADE/ANSEJ, CNAC, ANGEM, artisanal)
- Cadre II: CA forecast by activity type:
  - Production/vente: 5%
  - Services/other: 12%
  - Auto-entrepreneur: 0.5%
- Cadre III: Margin (only for regulated products)
- Total IFU = (A) + (B), minimum 30,000 DA (10,000 DA for AE)

**Verso:**
- Payment: intégral or fractionné (50% now, 25% Sept, 25% Dec)
- Amount in figures and words
- Cashier stamp

### Taux IFU
| Activity | Rate | Minimum |
|----------|------|---------|
| Production & vente | 5% | 30,000 DA |
| Services | 12% | 30,000 DA |
| Auto-entrepreneur | 0.5% | 10,000 DA |
| Recyclage papier/déchets | 5% | 30,000 DA |

### Penalties
| Delay | Majoration | Fine |
|-------|------------|------|
| < 1 month | 10% | 2,500 DA |
| 1-2 months | 20% | 5,000 DA |
| > 2 months | 25% | 10,000 DA |

### Official Downloads
- G12: https://www.mfdgi.gov.dz/files/516/-/13/GN12--12.pdf
- G12 BIS: https://www.mfdgi.gov.dz/files/516/-/994/G-12-Bis.pdf

### DSC Service: 3,000-8,000 DZD
- Fill G12 from client data
- Calculate IFU automatically
- Generate printable PDF
- Remind deadlines (G12 → G12 BIS cycle)

## 2. G50 — Déclaration Mensuelle (Série G N°50)

### What It Is
Monthly multi-tax declaration for businesses under régime réel. Combines TVA, IRG, IBS, and other taxes in one form.

### Who Must File
- All SARL, EURL, SPA, SNC under régime réel
- All professions libérales under régime réel
- Any employer paying salaries

### Taxes Declared
| Tax | Detail |
|-----|--------|
| TVA | Collectée (19%/9%) - Déductible = TVA nette |
| IRG Salaires | Total retenues IRG sur bulletins de paie |
| IBS | Acomptes provisionnels (trimestriels: mars, juin, nov) |
| Droit de timbre | 1% sur paiements en espèces |
| Retenues à la source | IRG sur loyers, honoraires, dividendes |

### Deadlines
- Monthly: before 20th of following month
- Even if no operations: must file "Néant"
- Payment not required at deposit time (but penalties apply)

### How to Fill
1. Page 1: Company info (NIF, article, raison sociale, address)
2. Tableau 1: TVA — total opérations imposables, TVA collectée, TVA déductible
3. Tableau 2: IBS acomptes (only in Feb, May, Oct)
4. Tableau 3: IRG salaires — total retenues, masse salariale brute
5. Tableau 4: Retenues à la source (loyers, honoraires)
6. Page 3: TVA detailed (HT par taux, TVA collectée)

### Deposit Methods
- **Jibayatic** (online, obligatory for régime réel since 2026)
- Paper: at inspection des impôts

### Penalties
| Situation | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| Late deposit (néant) | 500-1,500 DA |
| Late payment | 10% + 3%/month |
| No filing after mise en demeure | Up to 25% + taxation d'office |
| Late < 1 month | 30,000 DA |
| Late 1-2 months | 50,000 DA |
| Late > 2 months | 80,000 DA |
| False declaration | 1,000-10,000 DA per infraction |

### DSC Service: 5,000-15,000 DZD/month
- Prepare G50 from client's invoices + payroll
- Calculate TVA nette
- Generate printable form
- Monthly retainer: 5,000-10,000 DZD/month

## 3. Carte Grise — Immatriculation Véhicule

### What Changed (5 April 2026)
- New national digital system (SINNV)
- QR code on every new card
- All transactions at Daïra guichet (mairies no longer competent)
- Vendor + buyer must be physically present together
- Declaration of sale auto-generated by system

### Documents Required (Same Wilaya)
**Vendor:**
- CNIBE biométrique originale
- Carte grise originale (barrée au guichet)
- Certificat de résidence récent
- 2 photos d'identité

**Acheteur:**
- CNIBE biométrique originale
- Certificat de résidence récent (même wilaya)
- 2 photos d'identité

**Both:**
- Timbre fiscal (variable selon puissance)

### Documents (Different Wilaya)
Additional:
- Procès-verbal contrôle technique (visité par direction des mines)
- Fiche de renseignements (au guichet)
- Fiche de contrôle QR (wilaya d'origine)

### Documents (Imported Vehicle)
- Carte grise étrangère (+ traduction agréée si nécessaire)
- Document dédouanement (D10 ou quittance douane)
- Extrait de rôle apuré (NIF)
- Procès-verbal contrôle technique
- CNIBE ou passeport biométrique
- Certificat de résidence
- Photos + timbre fiscal

### Delays
| Case | Time |
|------|------|
| New car (concessionnaire) | 1-2 weeks |
| Used, same wilaya | 1-3 weeks |
| Used, different wilaya | 3-5 weeks |
| Imported vehicle | 2-6 weeks |

### Timbre Fiscal (2025)
- Starts at 800 DA (2-4 CV)
- Increases by power category

### DSC Service: 3,000-5,000 DZD
- Prepare complete dossier checklist
- Pre-fill forms
- Guide client through Daïra process
- Not a direct DSC service (must go to Daïra), but advisory + prep

## 4. CNAS — Caisse Nationale des Assurances Sociales (Salariés)

### What It Is
Social security for employees. Employer pays 26% of gross salary.

### Obligations
| Task | Deadline |
|------|----------|
| Affiliation new employee | Within 10 days of hire |
| Monthly declaration | Before end of following month |
| Annual salary declaration | Before 31 January |

### Taux (26% total)
| Part | Rate |
|------|------|
| Assurances sociales (maladie, maternité) | 12% |
| Retraite | 12.5% |
| Assurance décès | 1.5% |
| **Total** | **26%** |

### Key Services
- Affiliation salariés (SÉCU 01 form)
- Déclaration mensuelle des salaires
- Déclaration annuelle
- Carte CHIFA (santé) pour les salariés
- Déclaration accidents du travail

### Online
- Teledeclaration: https://teledeclaration.cnas.dz
- El-Hanaa portal (employees): https://elhanaa.cnas.dz

### DSC Service: 5,000-10,000 DZD/month retainer
- Prepare monthly CNAS declarations
- Affiliation new hires
- Annual salary declarations
- CHIFA requests

## 5. CASNOS — Caisse de Sécurité Sociale des Non-Salariés

### What It Is
Social security for self-employed, artisans, liberal professions, auto-entrepreneurs.

### Obligations
| Task | Deadline |
|------|----------|
| Affiliation | Within 10 days of activity start |
| Quarterly payment | End of following quarter |
| Annual declaration | Before 1 March |

### Taux (15% total)
| Part | Rate |
|------|------|
| Assurances sociales | 7.5% |
| Retraite | 7.5% |
| **Total** | **15%** |

### Assiette (2026)
- Minimum: SNMG annuel = 288,000 DZD/an → cotisation 43,200 DZD/an
- Maximum: 20 × SNMG = 5,760,000 DZD/an → cotisation 864,000 DZD/an
- Auto-entrepreneur: forfait 24,000 DZD/an

### Penalties
- 11% of amount due at 1st month delay
- +1% per additional month
- Annual declaration late: 162,000 DA penalty

### Documents Required
- Formulaire d'affiliation signé
- Copie RC (personne physique) ou RC + statuts (morale)
- Copie agrément/autorisation (activities réglementées)
- Certificat de existence DGI
- Carte artisan/agriculteur (if applicable)
- Copie CNI
- 2 photos

### Online
- E-Affiliation: https://damancom.casnos.dz
- Site: https://www.casnos.com.dz

### DSC Service: 3,000-8,000 DZD
- Prepare affiliation dossier
- Calculate quarterly payments
- Fill annual declaration
- Track deadlines

## 6. ANEM — Agence Nationale de l'Emploi

### What It Is
Employment agency. Job seekers register here. Employers post offers here.

### Key Services
- Inscription demandeur d'emploi (via WassitOnline)
- Prolongation demande d'emploi (6 months)
- Allocation chômage (via MINHA platform)
- Placement et orientation

### Online
- WassitOnline: https://wassitonline.anem.dz
- MINHA (allocation chômage): https://minha.anem.dz
- Site: https://www.anem.dz

### DSC Service: 1,000-3,000 DZD
- Help clients register on ANEM
- Prepare allocation chômage dossier
- Guide through MINHA platform

## 7. CNRC — Registre du Commerce (via Sidjilcom)

### What It Is
Official business registration. Every commercial activity needs a Registre du Commerce (RC).

### Online Creation (Sidjilcom)
1. Create account at www.sidjilcom.dz
2. Choose legal form (auto-entrepreneur, personne physique, personne morale)
3. Declare activity (code CNRC, address, denomination)
4. Upload documents (CNA, acte naissance, casier judiciaire, certificat résidence)
5. Pay fees (CIB online)
6. Receive RC électronique in 3-7 days

### Fees
| Form | Cost |
|------|------|
| Auto-entrepreneur | 1,500-2,000 DA |
| Entrepreneur individuel | ~3,000 DA |
| EURL/SARL | 5,000-8,000 DA |

### After RC
1. DGI → NIF (Numéro Identification Fiscale)
2. CASNOS → Affiliation
3. Banque → Compte professionnel
4. ONS → NIS (Numéro Identification Statistique)

### DSC Service: 5,000-15,000 DZD
- Full company creation package
- Guide through Sidjilcom
- Prepare all documents
- Handle DGI, CASNOS, ONS registrations

Tax identification number. Every business must have one.

## 9. Auto-Entrepreneur (ANAE)

### What It Is
Simplified status for solo entrepreneurs. CA < 8M DZD (updated from 5M).

### Costs (1st Year)
| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Carte ANAE | 1,200 DA |
| Frais postaux | ~100 DA |
| CASNOS forfait | 24,000 DA |
| IFU minimum | 10,000 DA |
| **Total** | **~35,000 DA** |

### Obligations
- Monthly CA declaration on anae.dz (before 20th)
- CASNOS payment (quarterly or annual)
- Registre simplifié recettes/dépenses
- Factures conformes with NIF + carte ANAE mention

### DSC Service: 10,000-20,000 DZD
- Full auto-entrepreneur creation package
- ANAE inscription
- CASNOS affiliation
- First month declarations
- Templates for invoices + registre
