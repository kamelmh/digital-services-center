## How to Use

### Step 1: Send Proposal to Client
1. Open `proposals/digital-marketing-proposal.md`
2. Replace `[Nom de l'entreprise]` with client name
3. Customize services based on their needs
4. Export to PDF or send as markdown

### Step 2: Post on Facebook
1. Open `marketing/facebook-post.html` in browser
2. Take screenshot (1200x630)
3. Copy caption from `marketing/facebook-posts.md`
4. Post on Facebook page

### Step 3: Track Results
- Monitor likes, comments, shares
- Track inquiries (DMs, calls)
- Measure conversion rate
- Adjust strategy monthly
