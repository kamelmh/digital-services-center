# Algerian Business Obligations — Annual Timeline

> When each form must be filed. DSC can send reminders and prepare forms in advance.

## Monthly Obligations

| Day | Form | Who | What |
|-----|------|-----|------|
| 20th | G50 | Companies with VAT | TVA/TAP/IRG monthly declaration |

## Annual Obligations

| Date | Form | Who | What |
|------|------|-----|------|
| January 20 | G12 bis | Auto-entrepreneurs | Final turnover declaration |
| January 31 | DAS (CNAS) | All employers | Annual salary declaration |
| March 1 | CASNOS CA | Self-employed | Turnover declaration |
| April 30 | G1/G2 | All taxpayers | Annual income tax return |
| April 30 | G13 | Liberal professions | Professional income declaration |
| June 30 | G12 | Auto-entrepreneurs | Forecast turnover declaration |
| June 30 | CASNOS payment | Self-employed | Social security payment |

## Startup Obligations (First 30 Days)

| Deadline | Form | Agency | What |
|----------|------|--------|------|
| Day 1-30 | G8 | DGI | Business start declaration (get NIF) |
| Day 1-10 | CASNOS affiliation | CASNOS | Self-employed social security enrollment |
| Before first hire | SECU 01 | CNRC | Employer social security enrollment |
| After RC | NIS | ONS | Statistical identification |

## DSC Reminder System (Planned)

- 30 days before deadline: Form preparation notification
- 14 days before deadline: Reminder + auto-fill available
- 7 days before deadline: Urgent reminder
- 1 day before deadline: Final alert

## Wilaya-Specific Notes

- Some wilayas have different processing times
- Paper filing at centre d'impot may require appointment
- Jibayatic online filing available 24/7
- CNAS teledeclaration deadline may differ from national
