# CNAS — Caisse Nationale des Assurances Sociales des Travailleurs Salariés

> Social security for salaried employees. Employers must register and contribute.

## Quick Facts

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Full name | Caisse Nationale des Assurances Sociales des Travailleurs Salariés |
| Arabic | الصندوق الوطني لتأمينات العمال الأجراء |
| Website | cnas.dz |
| Portal | teledeclaration.cnas.dz, elhana-cnas.dz |
| Jurisdiction | All salaried employees in Algeria |
| Contribution rate | 34.5% total (25% employer + 9% employee + 0.5% social works) |

## What CNAS Does

1. **Manages employee social security** — health, maternity, work injury
2. **Collects contributions** — 34.5% of gross salary
3. **Pays benefits** — sick leave, maternity, family allowances, pensions
4. **Maintains employee records** — DAS (annual salary declaration)
