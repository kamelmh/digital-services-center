# DGI — Direction Générale des Impôts

> Algeria's tax authority. Issues all tax forms, manages NIF issuance, oversees tax collection.

## Quick Facts

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Full name | Direction Générale des Impôts / الديوان العام للضرائب |
| Website | mfdgi.gov.dz |
| Portal | Jibayatic (jibayatic.mf.gov.dz) — online filing |
| Jurisdiction | All taxpayers in Algeria |
| Key function | Tax collection, NIF issuance, form distribution |

## What DGI Does

1. **Issues NIF** (Numéro d'Identification Fiscale) — every business/person needs this
2. **Collects taxes** — IRG, IBS, TVA, TAP, Timbre
3. **Provides forms** — all "Série G" forms are DGI forms
4. **Processes filings** — paper at tax office or electronic via Jibayatic
5. **Issues attestations** — tax clearance certificates, existence certificates
