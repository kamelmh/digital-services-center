# CASNOS — Caisse Nationale de Sécurité Sociale des Non-Salariés

> Social security for self-employed, freelancers, liberal professions. Every freelancer must register.

## Quick Facts

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Full name | Caisse Nationale de Sécurité Sociale des Non-Salariés |
| Arabic | الصندوق الوطني لتأمينات غير الأجراء |
| Website | casnos.dz |
| Portals | guichet.casnos.dz, damancom.casnos.dz |
| Jurisdiction | All self-employed, freelancers, liberal professions |
| Contribution rate | 15% of declared annual turnover |

## Who Must Register

- Freelancers (all types)
- Liberal professions (lawyers, doctors, consultants, accountants, engineers)
- Independent traders (without RC — otherwise CNAS applies)
- Artisans
- Auto-entrepreneurs (if not covered by CNAS)
- Farmers

**Deadline:** Within 10 days of starting activity

## Contribution Calculation

```
Annual contribution = 15% × Declared Annual Turnover
Minimum contribution = 15% × SNMG × 12 = 15% × 24,000 × 12 = 43,200 DZD/year
```

**Example:**
- Freelancer declares 2,000,000 DZD annual turnover
- Contribution = 15% × 2,000,000 = 300,000 DZD/year = 25,000 DZD/month

## What You Get

- Health insurance (medical care, hospitalization)
- Maternity benefits
- Retirement pension (after contribution period)
- Work injury coverage
- Family allowances
