# CNRC — Centre National du Registre du Commerce

> Commercial registration authority. Every business doing commercial activity must register.

## Quick Facts

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Full name | Centre National du Registre du Commerce / المركز الوطني للسجل التجاري |
| Website | commerce.gov.dz |
| Portal | SIDJILCOM (sidjilcom.cnrc.dz) — electronic filing |
| Jurisdiction | All commercial businesses in Algeria |
| Registration cost | 4,000 DA timbre fiscal |

## What CNRC Does

1. **Registers businesses** — issues RC (Registre du Commerce) number
2. **Reserves company names** — certificat négatif
3. **Maintains commercial records** — modifications, cancellations
4. **Issues extracts** — RC extracts (valid 2 years)

## Key Forms

| Form | Name | Purpose | Who |
|------|------|---------|-----|
| F1 | Immatriculation personne morale | RC for companies (SARL, EURL, SPA) | New companies |
| F2 | Immatriculation commerçant | RC for individual traders | Individual merchants |
| Certificat négatif | Dénomination | Company name reservation | New companies |
| Modification | Demande de modification | RC update (address, activity, manager) | Existing businesses |
| Radiation | Demande de radiation | RC closure | Closing businesses |
| Duplicata | Demande de duplicata | RC duplicate extract | Lost RC |

## Required Documents (F1 — Company)

1. Statutes (notarized for SARL/EURL)
2. NIF (from DGI — G8 form)
3. Manager's ID (carte nationale)
4. Lease contract or property proof
5. Casier judiciaire n°3 (criminal record)
6. 4,000 DA timbre fiscal
7. Certificat négatif (name reservation)
