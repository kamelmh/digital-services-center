# ONS — Office National des Statistiques

> National statistics office. Issues NIS (Numéro d'Identification Statistique) for all businesses.

## Quick Facts

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Full name | Office National des Statistiques / المكتب الوطني للإحصائيات |
| Website | ons.dz |
| Jurisdiction | All businesses in Algeria |
| Key output | NIS (Numéro d'Identification Statistique) |

## What ONS Does

1. **Issues NIS** — statistical identification number for all businesses
2. **Collects economic data** — surveys, censuses
3. **Maintains business registry** — statistical classification

## NIS — Numéro d'Identification Statistique

**What it's for:**
- Bank account opening
- CNAS/CASNOS affiliation
- Public tenders
- Import/export procedures
- Government contracts

**How to get it:**
1. Register at CNRC first (get RC number)
2. Apply at regional ONS office
3. Submit: RC copy, NIF copy, application form
4. Receive NIS certificate (usually same day or next day)

**Format:** XX-XXX-XXX-X (10 digits with dashes)
