# Digital Services Center — Business Plan

## Executive Summary

A "Centre de Services Numériques" (Digital Services Center) that helps people with:
- Tax declarations and compliance
- Government paperwork (online and offline)
- Document preparation (CVs, letters, forms)
- Printing, scanning, copying
- Bill payments
- Vehicle registration and fines
- And more...

**Location:** 2nd floor apartment, above existing store in El Bayadh
**Legal Status:** Auto-Entrepreneur (ANAE)
**Startup Cost:** 200,000 - 400,000 DZD (minimal — using existing computer + printer)
**Monthly Target:** 150,000 - 300,000 DZD revenue

---

## The Opportunity

### Why This Business Works in Algeria

1. **Government digitization is accelerating** — More services going online every year
2. **Digital divide persists** — Many people don't have computers, printers, or internet at home
3. **Complex bureaucracy** — People need help navigating tax forms, CNAS, CAF, etc.
4. **High demand** — Every neighborhood needs this service
5. **Low competition** — Only a few "écrivains publics" in El Bayadh

### What People Need Help With

| Service | Demand Level | Price Range |
|---------|--------------|-------------|
| Tax declarations (G12, G50) | HIGH | 1,000 - 3,000 DZD |

## Key Success Factors

### 1. Speed
- Quick services (under 5 min): immediate
- Document services (under 30 min): while-you-wait
- Admin services (over 30 min): next-day pickup

### 2. Reliability
- Always have backup internet (mobile hotspot)
- Save all work to cloud + local backup
- Never lose customer data

### 3. Customer Service
- Greet every customer with "Bonjour" / "السلام عليكم"
- Explain prices clearly before starting
- Offer tea/water to waiting customers

### 4. Location
- Clear signboard visible from street
- Easy access (ground floor or elevator)
- Parking nearby
